Transient notification for completed or failed actions (bottom-right, 360px).

```jsx
<ToastViewport><Toast tone="success" title="Đã lưu báo giá" description="BG-2026-0412 · phiên bản 3" /></ToastViewport>
```

Also exports ToastViewport (fixed bottom-right stack). Use `loading` while a transaction is pending.
